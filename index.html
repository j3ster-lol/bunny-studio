How do I place an order?+

Join the BUNNY community server and contact the team through the available ticket or support system. Choose the service you are interested in and provide a short description of your request.

Are the delivery times guaranteed?+

No. The timeframes on this website are maximum target estimates. The actual schedule depends on the request, its complexity, availability and the details confirmed with you before work begins.

Can I request something that is not listed?+

Yes, you can ask. Describe the result you have in mind and the team can review whether it is something we can help with. A request is not confirmed until its scope and availability have been discussed.

Can I ask for a custom Discord bot?+

Absolutely. Share what you want the bot to do, which server features matter most and whether you have any examples. Clear requirements help us understand the project and estimate the work.

What information should I include?+

Include the service name, your main goal, important features or design preferences, and any deadline that matters. For development requests, a short list of required functions is especially useful.

What if I need help after delivery?+

Reach out through the support channel on our community server and include the details of your order. The team can review your question and explain what support is available for the delivered service.

Do you start work immediately?+

Work begins after the request has been reviewed and the relevant details, availability and terms have been confirmed. This helps avoid misunderstandings and keeps the process organised.

Where can I see service availability?+

Availability can change, especially for services with limited stock. Contact the team before ordering so the current status can be confirmed.
